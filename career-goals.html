CAREER GOALS PORTFOLIO

A roadmap from learner to business leader

My career plan is designed to build experience in real business settings, strengthen technical and people skills, and gradually move from contributing to projects toward leading ventures and teams.

01–02
YEARS
SHORT-TERM GOALS

Build real-world business experience

  • Secure an internship or entry-level experience in marketing, business analysis, operations, or finance.
  • Complete at least one practical business certification or focused online course.
  • Build a small side venture or project to practice customer discovery, budgeting, branding, and sales.
  • Strengthen Excel, presentation, communication, and professional networking skills.
03–05
YEARS
MID-TERM GOALS

Become a high-value business professional

  • Grow into a business analyst, project coordinator, marketing specialist, or management-track role.
  • Lead projects that improve revenue, customer experience, efficiency, or market growth.
  • Develop a stronger professional network through mentors, industry events, and collaborative work.
  • Evaluate graduate study or an MBA once work experience clarifies the most valuable specialization.
07–10
YEARS
LONG-TERM GOALS

Lead or build a scalable company

  • Move into a senior leadership or entrepreneurship role with responsibility for people, strategy, and performance.
  • Launch or lead a business in a growth-oriented industry, using data and customer insight to guide decisions.
  • Build a reputation for ethical leadership, innovation, and sustainable value creation.
  • Mentor younger professionals and help create opportunities for others as my experience grows.
PREFERRED INDUSTRY / DOMAIN

E-commerce & Technology-Enabled Business

I am especially interested in e-commerce, digital services, retail innovation, and other business models where technology can improve customer access, operational efficiency, and growth.
